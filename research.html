RESEARCH

Research Overview

My research interests lie in Applied Probability, with a particular focus on Queueing Theory in Heavy Traffic and Stochastic Control. In recent years, I have worked extensively on Matching Queue Systems, many of which are motivated by practical problems arising in Industrial Engineering and Operations Research.

I am also interested in Mathematical Finance, especially asset pricing problems and the dynamics of the Limit Order Book (LOB). More recently, I have developed interests in queue control problems via Reinforcement Learning (RL), RL for solving market-making problems in high-frequency trading, and fractional stochastic control problems.

Selected Research Projects

The following are selected ongoing and completed research projects in stochastic systems, queueing theory, mathematical finance, stochastic control, and reinforcement learning.

Dynamics of Limit Order Books in High-Frequency Trading

Fall 2023 – Present
Postdoctoral Mentor: Dr. José E. Figueroa-López, Washington University in St. Louis
Joint work with Dr. Jonathan Chávez, University of Rhode Island
  • Construct a matching-system framework for Limit Order Books (LOBs) and develop algorithms for the optimal placement problem faced by market makers.
  • Study the performance of reinforcement learning methods for solving market-making problems with inventory risk.
  • Train market-making agents using reinforcement learning in realistic market environments constructed from historical Limit Order Book data.
  • Use neural networks to approximate state-value and state-action value functions and compare their performance with tile coding and with theoretical solutions to the optimal placement problem.

Joint Stochastic Control on Product and Process Innovations

Spring 2025 – Present
  • Study product and process innovation as key mechanisms for quality improvement and cost reduction, particularly in systems where dynamic demand and resource recycling affect operational efficiency.
  • Explore stochastic control strategies within a propagation-of-chaos framework. Study the joint control of product pricing and innovation under constraints involving recycling rates and demand propagation.
  • Integrate rigorous stochastic analysis to develop adaptive and computationally tractable control strategies for complex systems, which are applicable to the joint optimization of product and process innovations in monopoly models.

Scaling Limits on Regulated Matching Systems

Fall 2024 – Present
Joint work with Dr. Souvik Ray and Dr. Ruoyu Wu, Iowa State University
  • Construct diffusion approximations for large-scale regulated matching systems, characterized by coupled regulated stochastic integral equations.
  • Develop a non-trivial approach for establishing existence and uniqueness of weak solutions to the non-Lipschitz coupled regulated stochastic integral equations and numerically simulate the resulting matching behavior.
  • Develop control strategies for heterogeneous matching systems, especially under resource constraints requiring admission-control or buffer-management policies.

Multi-Component Matching Queue Systems in Heavy Traffic

Summer 2019 – 2022
Advisor: Dr. Ananda Weerasinghe, Iowa State University
  • Constructed many-component matching queue models and studied their diffusion-scaled heavy-traffic limits, which are characterized by coupled nonlinear stochastic integral equations.
  • Established convergence results for cost functionals associated with the matching queue systems.
  • Formulated an asymptotic relationship between queue-length processes and virtual waiting-time processes, yielding an asymptotic version of Little's law, and further refined the result in the Lp sense on a suitable probability space.
  • Simulated the matching queue systems and their corresponding stochastic integral equations numerically in Python.
  • Noted that the matching queue models without abandonment may be interpreted in connection with basket option pricing problems.
